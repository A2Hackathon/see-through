This is our (Albert and Amanda's) project for **HopHacks '26**!!

In today’s urban environments, many aspects of our surroundings are designed without fully considering the challenges faced by people who are blind or visually impaired. Everyday tasks that many of us take for granted, from avoiding an unexpected obstacle to recognizing a familiar person, can require significant effort.

As AI-generated voices become increasingly convincing, it can be difficult to know whether the person speaking is actually someone you trust. For people who are visually impaired and rely heavily on auditory cues, this can create new safety risks.

This led us to the idea of a lightweight visual aid that can attach directly to a pair of glasses, using computer vision to quietly interpret the user's surroundings and communicate important information in real time.

Purpose of app: 
- Obstacle & Hazard Avoidance: Real-time path scanning for physical hazards.
- Approach Alerts: Proximity detection for incoming individuals.
- Known Face Identification: Instant identification of recognized acquaintances.
